# Responsible AI — Principles for Enterprise Deployment

## The Core Principles

### 1. Fairness
The system treats similar situations similarly, regardless of protected attributes.

### 2. Accountability
Someone is responsible for every decision the system makes.

### 3. Transparency
Users know they're interacting with AI and understand its capabilities and limits.

### 4. Privacy
User data is protected, used only for stated purposes, and can be deleted on request.

### 5. Safety
The system is tested for harmful outputs and has guardrails against misuse.

### 6. Reliability
The system works consistently and gracefully handles failures.

### 7. Human Oversight
Humans can intervene, override, and shut down the system when needed.

## Enterprise Implementation
- Every principle needs technical controls (not just policies)
- Every principle needs measurements (bias metrics, safety scores)
- Every principle needs governance (who owns it, how it's audited)
- Every principle needs incident response (what happens when it's violated)

## The Responsible AI Program Structure
- Executive sponsor (VP or C-level)
- Responsible AI committee (cross-functional review)
- Technical AI ethics team (day-to-day operations)
- Every product team has a Responsible AI champion
